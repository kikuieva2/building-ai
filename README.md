# AI Real Estate Recommendation Platform

## Summary

This project is an AI-powered real estate platform that helps users find properties that match their needs, budget, and lifestyle preferences.

The platform uses machine learning algorithms to analyze housing data and recommend apartments or houses based on the user's interests.

---

## Background

Finding the right property can be difficult and time-consuming. People often spend hours searching through advertisements that do not match their preferences.

Many users struggle with:
- finding affordable housing
- comparing different properties
- understanding market prices
- choosing the best location

This project aims to simplify the real estate search process using artificial intelligence.

---

## How is it used?

Users enter their preferences such as:
- budget
- location
- apartment size
- number of rooms
- distance from city center
- nearby schools or transport

The AI system analyzes the data and suggests the most suitable properties.

The platform could also:
- predict property prices
- detect overpriced apartments
- recommend investment opportunities
- analyze market trends

Possible data sources:

## Challenges

The platform may face challenges such as:
- inaccurate or outdated listings
- changing market prices
- limited data availability

AI recommendations may not always perfectly match personal preferences.
